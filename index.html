About Us

Premium Badminton Experience

JetBlast is a state-of-the-art three-court badminton stadium in Kochchikade, Negombo, open 24/7 for players who never stop loving the game. Designed with world-class PVC flooring and professional lighting, our courts provide the ideal setting for both casual rallies and competitive matches. We ensure your comfort and safety with clean washrooms, ample parking, and a secure, well-lit environment around the clock.

More than just a place to play, JetBlast is a hub for badminton enthusiasts — a space to train, compete, connect, and enjoy the sport at any hour of the day. Whether you're sharpening your skills, organizing a tournament, or simply playing for fun, JetBlast delivers the energy, quality, and community spirit to keep your game alive.
